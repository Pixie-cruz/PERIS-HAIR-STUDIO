            About the collective

            Peris Style Collective is a small team of stylists who believe good style is a skill anyone can learn, not a talent you're born with.

            We started by helping friends clear out overflowing wardrobes. Now we work with busy professionals, brides, parents and anyone who's tired of having "nothing to wear" in a full closet.

            Every session is one-to-one and unhurried. We listen first, then style around your budget, your body and your daily life.

            Questions and answers

            How long is a session?

            It depends on the service. A consultation takes about 45 minutes and a personal shopping day takes around four hours. Each service lists its time.

            Do I need to pay a deposit?

            Yes. A deposit holds your slot and comes off your total. You pay the rest on the day.

            What if I need to reschedule?

            Tell us at least 24 hours before your session and we'll move it for free. Later changes may lose the deposit.

            What should I bring?

            For a wardrobe edit, nothing: we work with your clothes at home. For a consultation, bring photos of outfits you love and a rough budget.

            Do you style men, women and children?

            Yes. We style everyone. Tell us who the session is for when you book.

            Can you come to me?

            Yes, for wardrobe edits and bridal styling. Ask us about travel when you book.
